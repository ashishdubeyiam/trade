## Literature Review

### 1. Introduction to Financial Fraud and its Detection

Financial fraud encompasses a broad range of deceptive practices involving intentional misrepresentation or manipulation for the purpose of illicit financial gain, impacting both institutions and individuals with significant economic losses and an erosion of trust in financial systems. The detection of such activities presents persistent challenges due to the highly imbalanced nature of transactional data, where fraudulent instances are vastly outnumbered by legitimate ones, coupled with the adaptive and ever-evolving tactics employed by fraudsters seeking to circumvent established security measures. Consequently, there is a continuous and critical need for rapid, robust, and intelligent fraud detection systems capable of identifying suspicious activities promptly to mitigate potential damages.
